# Feedback, complaints and reviews

Covers formal complaints and product reviews.

## Making a complaint
Complaints can be raised in chat, by email, or from Orders, Report a problem.
Every complaint gets a reference beginning SA- and an acknowledgement within one
business day. A full response follows within five working days.

## What happens next
A case handler reviews the order history, courier records and any photographs
provided. Photographs help considerably for damage and wrong-item complaints.
Outcomes are a replacement, a refund, a partial refund, or an explanation of why
no remedy applies.

## Escalating a complaint
If the outcome is unsatisfactory, request a review by the complaints team within
30 days of the decision. That review is final internally. Customers in the EU
and UK may then take the matter to an alternative dispute resolution scheme at
no cost.

## Reviews
Verified purchasers can leave a product review for 90 days after delivery.
Reviews are published within 48 hours after an automated check. Reviews are not
removed for being negative; they are removed only for abusive language, personal
data, or content unrelated to the product.
