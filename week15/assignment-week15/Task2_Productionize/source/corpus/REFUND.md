# Refunds

Covers the refund policy and tracking a refund.

## Policy
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Faulty items can be returned within 12 months. Perishable goods, personalised
items and opened hygiene products cannot be returned unless faulty. Original
delivery charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

## How to return
Start from Orders, Return item, and print the prepaid label. Returns are free
for faulty or incorrect items; otherwise 2.99 is deducted from the refund for
the label. Items must be in their original packaging where practical.

## Timing
Refunds are issued within three working days of the return arriving at the
warehouse. The money then takes a further three to five working days to appear,
depending on the bank. Refunds always go back to the original payment method;
they cannot be redirected to a different card or account.

## Tracking a refund
Orders, followed by the order, then Refund status, shows received, approved and
issued, each with a timestamp. Once issued, a reference number is shown, and the
bank can trace the payment with it.

## Refunds taking too long
If a refund was issued more than ten working days ago and has not appeared,
contact support with the reference and the case is raised with the processor.
