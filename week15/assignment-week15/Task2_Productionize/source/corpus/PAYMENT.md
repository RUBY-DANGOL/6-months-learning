# Payments

Covers accepted payment methods and payment problems.

## Accepted methods
Visa, Mastercard and American Express; PayPal; Apple Pay and Google Pay; and
ShopAssist gift cards. Bank transfer is available on business accounts over
250.00. Cash on delivery is not offered.

## Failed payments
The three usual causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, and a bank
fraud block on an unfamiliar merchant. The bank rarely tells us which. Retry
with another method, or call the bank and ask them to allow the transaction.

## Duplicate charges
Seeing two charges for one order is usually an authorisation hold alongside the
real charge. Holds disappear on their own within three to five working days. If
both are still present after five working days, contact support with the dates
and amounts and the extra charge is refunded.

## Currency
Prices are charged in the store currency shown at checkout. Cards in another
currency are converted by the issuing bank at its own rate, and that conversion
is outside our control.

## Saved cards
Cards are stored with the payment processor, never on ShopAssist servers.
Remove a saved card under Settings, Payment methods. Removing a card does not
affect orders already placed.
