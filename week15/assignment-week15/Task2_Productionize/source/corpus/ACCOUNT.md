# Account

Covers creating, editing, switching and deleting a ShopAssist account.

## Creating an account
Sign up with an email address and a password of at least ten characters. A
verification link is sent immediately and expires after 24 hours. If it expires,
request a new one from the sign-in page; the old link stops working.

## Editing details
Name, email, phone number and marketing preferences can be changed from
Settings, Account details. Changing the email address sends a confirmation to
both the old and the new address, and the change only takes effect once the new
address is confirmed.

## Switching between accounts
Multiple accounts can be linked to one device. Use Settings, Switch account.
Business and personal accounts cannot be merged; orders stay with the account
that placed them.

## Deleting an account
Deletion is requested from Settings, Close account. The account is deactivated
at once and permanently erased after 30 days, during which signing in cancels
the deletion. Orders still in transit block deletion until they are delivered or
cancelled. Invoices are retained for seven years for tax purposes even after
deletion, as legally required.
