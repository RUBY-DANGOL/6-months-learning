# Delivery options

Covers how orders are shipped, what each option costs, and delivery windows.

## Options and cost
Standard delivery takes three to five working days and is free on orders over
40.00, otherwise 3.99. Express delivery is next working day for orders placed
before 18:00 and costs 6.99. Same-day delivery is available in selected cities
for 9.99 on orders placed before 11:00.

## Collection points
Orders can be sent to a collection point instead of an address for 2.49. Parcels
are held for seven days, after which they are returned to the warehouse and
refunded automatically.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse and updates
roughly every four hours. Tracking that has not moved for 48 hours usually means
the parcel is held at a depot; contact support and a trace is opened.

## Missed deliveries
Couriers attempt delivery twice. After a second failure the parcel goes to the
nearest collection point and the customer is notified by SMS. Delivery to a
neighbour or a safe place can be set as a preference before dispatch.

## Delays
Weather, strikes and customs can add delay. Orders more than ten working days
late are treated as lost and are replaced or refunded at the customer's choice.
