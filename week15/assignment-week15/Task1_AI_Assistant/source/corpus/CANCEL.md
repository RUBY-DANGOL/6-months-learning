# Cancellation fees

Covers what it costs to cancel an order or a service.

## When cancellation is free
Orders cancelled before the warehouse marks them as picked cost nothing. That is
normally the first two hours after the order is placed, and up to 24 hours for
made-to-order items.

## When a fee applies
Once an order has been picked and packed, cancelling carries a restocking fee of
5 percent of the order value, capped at 15.00. Orders already handed to the
courier cannot be cancelled and must be returned instead, under the refund
policy.

## Subscription cancellations
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time with no fee and stay active until the
end of the paid period. Annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase are
refunded in full. After 14 days an annual plan is refunded pro rata, less an
administration fee of 10 percent of the remaining balance.

## Checking a fee before committing
The exact fee for a specific order is shown in Orders, Cancel order, before the
cancellation is confirmed. No fee is charged until that confirmation.
