# Contacting support

Covers reaching customer service and escalating to a human agent.

## Channels and hours
Live chat runs 24 hours a day. Phone support is open 08:00 to 20:00 local time,
seven days a week. Email is answered within one business day.

## Reaching a human agent
Ask for a human at any point in a chat and the conversation is transferred to
the next available advisor. Median wait is under three minutes on weekdays and
can reach fifteen minutes at weekends and during sale periods.

## Escalation
If an issue is not resolved on first contact, request escalation to a team lead.
Escalated cases carry a response commitment of four working hours. A case
reference beginning SA- is issued and can be quoted on any channel to resume
without repeating the history.

## What to have ready
The order number, the email address on the account, and a short description of
what happened. For payment problems, the last four digits of the card only;
advisors never ask for a full card number, a PIN, or a password.
