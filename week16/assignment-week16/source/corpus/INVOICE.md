# Invoices

Covers finding, downloading and correcting invoices.

## Where invoices live
Every completed order has an invoice under Orders, followed by the order, then
Invoice. Invoices become available once the order ships, not when it is placed.
They download as PDF and can be emailed to any address from the same screen.

## Business invoices
A VAT number and company name can be added under Settings, Billing details.
Invoices issued after that change carry the VAT details. Invoices already issued
cannot be edited, but a corrected replacement can be requested within 60 days of
the original.

## Missing invoices
If an order shipped more than 24 hours ago and no invoice appears, it usually
means the payment has not fully settled. Support can confirm and issue the
invoice manually.

## Bulk download
Accounts with more than twenty orders can export a date range as a single ZIP
from Settings, Billing, Export invoices. The export is emailed as a link that
expires after seven days.

## Retention
Invoices remain available for seven years, including after an account is closed.
