# Orders

Covers placing, changing and cancelling orders.

## Placing an order
Items stay in the basket for 30 days but are not reserved; stock is only held
once payment is authorised. Order confirmation is emailed within a few minutes.
If no confirmation arrives within an hour the order almost certainly did not
complete, and any pending charge is released by the bank within three to five
working days.

## Changing an order
Delivery address, delivery option and quantities can be changed while the order
still shows as Processing, normally the first two hours. Once it shows Picked,
nothing can be changed and the order must be cancelled or returned instead.
Items cannot be added to an existing order; place a second order.

## Cancelling an order
Cancel from Orders, Cancel order. Cancelling before picking is free. After
picking a restocking fee may apply, as set out in the cancellation fee policy.
Orders with the courier cannot be cancelled.

## Split shipments
Orders containing items from different warehouses may arrive in separate
parcels on different days at no extra cost. Each parcel has its own tracking
link, and the invoice still covers the whole order.

## Pre-orders
Pre-ordered items are charged when they ship, not when ordered, and can be
cancelled free of charge at any point before dispatch.
