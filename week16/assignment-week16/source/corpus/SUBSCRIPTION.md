# Newsletter subscription

Covers subscribing to and unsubscribing from ShopAssist emails.

## Subscribing
Subscribe from the footer of any page or under Settings, Communication
preferences. A confirmation email is sent and the subscription is only active
once the link in it is clicked, which is required under GDPR.

## What is sent
The weekly newsletter carries new arrivals, price drops and a subscriber-only
discount code. Frequency can be changed to monthly under Communication
preferences. Order confirmations, dispatch notices and refund notifications are
transactional and are always sent regardless of newsletter settings.

## Unsubscribing
Use the unsubscribe link at the foot of any newsletter, or turn it off under
Communication preferences. Unsubscribing takes effect within 24 hours; a
newsletter already queued may still arrive.

## Not receiving the newsletter
The usual causes are an unconfirmed subscription, a spam filter, or a bounce
that suspended the address. Two hard bounces suspend an address automatically,
and support can reinstate it.

## Data
Email addresses are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Full
detail is in the privacy policy.
